## Plan 1–4 backlog

### Plan 1 — Autoresearcher Research Synthesis

Turn Autoresearcher output into clean research synthesis: explain what changed, what it means, what to test next, and what to carry into the next cycle.

### Plan 2 — Chat Rail Context Switching

Make chat-rail context switch cleanly across routes, sessions, and work modes without losing the active conversational thread.

### Plan 3 — DSPy × Autoresearcher Integration

Connect DSPy optimization and signature-based evaluation to the Autoresearcher loop so synthesis, mutation, and scoring can inform one another.

### Plan 4 — Agentless Mechanistic Strategies

Add deterministic, agentless strategy patterns that are explicitly mechanistic and do not require a live agent in the loop.
