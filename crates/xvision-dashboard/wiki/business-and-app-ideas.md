# Business and App Ideas

This page collects product ideas and app concepts from the 2026-05-29 research drop.

## Ideas

### TradingView

- *Agentic charts with prompt query* — let users ask natural-language questions about a chart, then have an agent inspect the active market context, annotate the chart, and answer with a concise trading-oriented explanation.
- The interface should feel like a chart-first copilot: the chart stays primary, while the prompt query becomes the control surface for analysis, scenario questions, and follow-up exploration.
- Good follow-up features could include chart overlays, pattern summaries, watchlist-aware prompts, and a quick path from question to backtest or strategy draft.
